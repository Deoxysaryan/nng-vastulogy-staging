✦How I read your space

Most Vastu stops at a compass.
I read the whole mandala.

Classical Vastu reads every plot as a grid of 81 squares, called padas, each held by a devta, with Brahma at the centre. I read your plan on that grid, then with the people who live in it, so every correction fits your life.

8 directions and 16 zones, each ruled by its devta: Kubera in the north, Ishana in the north-east, Agni in the south-east.

Water in the north-east, fire in the south-east, earth in the south-west, air in the north-west, space at the centre.

Your boundary has 32 padas. I check the exact one your main door falls in, not just the side it faces.

The nine squares at the centre belong to Brahma. They hold the whole space, so I keep them open and light.

✦Vastu, explained

The questions I’m asked most.

From the main door to the Brahmasthan.

01

What is Vastulogy?

Vastu Shastra is India’s classical science of space. It reads every plot as the Vastu Purusha mandala: 81 squares, each held by a devta, with Brahma at the centre. Vastulogy is how I read your home, office or plot on that grid, along with the people who use it.

02

Which direction is best for the main door?

North, east and north-east are favoured most. A south or west door is not a verdict on your home. I check which of the 32 boundary squares your door falls in, what stands opposite it and how the entrance is lit.

03

What is the Brahmasthan, and why does it matter?

It is the centre of your home, held by Brahma. Keep it open, light and free of heavy furniture, pillars, stores or a toilet. A blocked centre is one of the first things I look for.

04

Where should the kitchen and stove be?

The south-east belongs to Agni, the fire element, so the stove sits best there. Keep the stove and the sink apart, because fire and water clash.

05

I have a toilet in the north-east. Is that serious?

The north-east, Ishanya, is the zone of water, prayer and clarity, so Vastu marks a toilet there down most often. I first correct around it with colour, placement and how the room is kept, without any building work.

06

Do I need to break walls? I live on rent.

No. I correct through placement, colour, objects and how you use a room, and I keep every change reversible, so it suits a rented flat or office too.

07

What should I keep ready?

A floor plan with north marked, or a simple sketch with room sizes. Photos of each room, the entrance and the road outside help too.

08

What does a consultation cost?

It depends on the space and its size. My team shares the fee when they call you. Sessions are online or in person in Noida, by appointment.
